About ULC Project

The ULC Project aims to create a decentralized platform where content creators can publish, share, and monetize their work without intermediaries. We believe in empowering creators and fostering a vibrant community through blockchain technology.

Key Features

Decentralized Network

Content is stored and managed on a distributed network, ensuring censorship resistance and data integrity.

Creator Monetization

Direct payment and revenue sharing models for creators, cutting out unnecessary third parties.

Community Governance

Token holders have a say in the platform's development and future direction.

Transparency

All transactions and content metadata are recorded on the blockchain for ultimate transparency.
