About ULC

The Universal Lightning Chain (ULC) provides a highly scalable and secure blockchain platform for decentralized applications. Our mission is to empower developers and users with a robust and efficient infrastructure.

ULC utilizes cutting-edge consensus mechanisms to ensure rapid transaction finality and low fees, making it ideal for a wide range of use cases from DeFi to gaming.
